      FAQ
      
Questions, answered.

      

        
What does a social media marketing retainer include?
It is scoped around your volume and goals: strategy and content pillars, a content calendar, designed posts and carousels, Reels and stories, scheduling and publishing, comment and DM handling, a weekly performance review and a monthly report. You get a written scope listing exactly what is included before anything starts.

        
How much does social media marketing cost?
Social media retainers start from ₹25,000/month, scoped on volume: how many posts, Reels and stories you need each month and how much production each one takes. Indian projects are quoted in rupees and international projects in US dollars. Every quote is written against your brief rather than a fixed package.

        
Do you guarantee followers, views or sales?
No. Nobody can honestly guarantee virality, follower counts or sales. What we commit to is the work: a clear strategy, consistent high-quality content, and weekly measurement that shows what is working so we can do more of it.

        
How does a project start?
Send your requirements over WhatsApp, email or the proposal form. We come back with scope, approach and a written quote against your brief. Nothing starts until that is agreed. If you prefer to talk first, you can book a ₹99 discovery call.

        
Who owns the content and the accounts?
You do. Your accounts stay in your name and the final content we produce for you is yours to use. Specific usage terms are written into your proposal.

        
How do approvals work?
Content is planned on a calendar you can see ahead of time, and posts go out after your approval. The approval rhythm, weekly batches or per-post, is agreed at the start.

        
How soon will we see results?
It depends on your starting point, audience and posting volume, so we don't promise a date. You will see the work on schedule from the first month, and each weekly review shows which content is moving reach, saves, profile visits and enquiries.

        
Which platforms do you manage?
Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, LinkedIn, X and WhatsApp Channels. We recommend where to focus based on where your buyers actually are.

        
Do you build websites, apps and automation too?
Yes. 3D and premium websites start from ₹25,000 and web and mobile applications from ₹50,000. We also build AI agents, business automation, branding and data analytics. Every quote is written against your brief.

        
How do support tickets work?
You create a ticket in this app and send it to us through Gmail, WhatsApp or SMS. On the live site it is also saved to our system so you can track its status and see our replies.

        
Where are you based and who do you work with?
We are based in Faridabad, Haryana, India and work remotely with businesses across India and worldwide, including fashion designers, interior designers, architects, real-estate agents, cafés, jewellery brands, startups and e-commerce businesses.
